Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

Account sign-in

The login page uses Firebase Authentication for email-and-password sign-in and password reset. Your password is processed in your browser and sent directly to Google Firebase over HTTPS; the FoodBridge hosting server does not receive or store it. Firebase may process your email address and technical information to provide and protect authentication. A signed-in session is kept in your browser until you sign out or it expires. Review Firebase privacy and security information.

Users can register publicly and must verify their email. Requested roles require administrator approval before the donor, kitchen, or volunteer workspaces are available. Do not use a password you use for another service.

Use a device you control and sign out before leaving a shared device.

Food listings and contact details

Approved users can store food listing and request details in Firebase Cloud Firestore. Exact pickup addresses and donor phone numbers are stored separately from browseable listings and are made available only to the donor, the kitchen after a request is accepted, and the volunteer assigned to that delivery. Do not add payment, government ID, health, or other sensitive personal information to listing notes.

Contact the FoodBridge administrator to request correction or deletion of your account data. The administrator should remove associated Firebase Authentication and Firestore records when appropriate.

Information processed when you visit

The website is hosted by Render as a static site. Render and its delivery infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP address, requested page, and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. Consult Render's current privacy information for its handling and retention practices.

Third-party services

The site loads Firebase's JavaScript SDK from Google's Firebase hosting and connects to Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. It does not intentionally use advertising or analytics services.

Food safety and service limits

FoodBridge helps coordinate participant-submitted listings and handoffs. Donors are responsible for the accuracy and safe handling of their food. FoodBridge does not independently inspect food or guarantee that a listing, recipient, or delivery is safe. Have this notice reviewed for the intended jurisdiction before public launch.
