Website Notice

Participant responsibilities

FoodBridge provides tools for posting food listings, requesting food, and coordinating delivery tasks. Donors are responsible for the food's condition, handling, ingredients, allergens, and accuracy of listing details. Community kitchens and volunteers are responsible for deciding whether they can safely receive and transport food. A FoodBridge listing or status is not a food-safety inspection or guarantee.

Not legal, tax, or food-safety advice

Content about food handling, statutory protections, or tax matters is general information only. It may be incomplete or out of date and is not a substitute for advice from qualified legal, tax, public-health, or food-safety professionals. Confirm requirements with the relevant authorities before acting.

No reliance for donation decisions

Participants must coordinate pickup and delivery directly and follow applicable food-safety and local requirements. FoodBridge does not provide transport, guarantee a handoff, issue tax receipts, or determine whether a donation is tax-deductible.

Account and access

New accounts must verify their email and receive approval for a requested role. Accounts may be suspended or removed to protect participants. Users must keep their credentials private and provide accurate account and listing information.
